Privacy Policy for Shikaku Puzzle

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

Introduction

Shikaku Puzzle ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use our puzzle game.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Shikaku Puzzle does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information.

This app is designed to be completely private and offline-first. All game data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • ❌ Usage analytics or telemetry
  • ❌ Crash reports
  • ❌ Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • ❌ Contacts or calendar data
  • ❌ Any other personal or sensitive information

Local Data Storage

The app uses your device's local storage (localStorage) to save:

  • ✅ Your puzzle solving history
  • ✅ Game statistics (puzzles solved, scores, time played)
  • ✅ User preferences (ink color selection)
  • ✅ Game state (current puzzle progress)

Important: This data is stored ONLY on your device and is never transmitted to any external server or third party. If you uninstall the app, this data will be deleted.

No Third-Party Services

Shikaku Puzzle does not use:

  • No advertising networks
  • No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No social media integrations
  • No cloud backup services
  • No external APIs or web services

The app functions completely offline and does not require an internet connection to play.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Shikaku Puzzle is suitable for children of all ages and is designed to be safe for use by children.

Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not collect personal information from users of any age
  • No parental consent is required because no data is collected

Parents can confidently allow their children to use this app without concern for privacy or data collection.

Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

  • Storage/Files: Only used to save game progress locally on your device. No files are uploaded or shared.
  • No other permissions are requested or required

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted:

  • There is no data to be compromised in a data breach
  • All game data remains on your device under your control
  • You can delete all app data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
